Fig. 1. Dual-branch framework for evaluating catchment-wise architecture selection. (a) Regional inputs and library of n=10 independently trained instances (random seeds) for the baseline (B0) and two candidates (M1, EA). (b) Single-instance evaluation (k=1): early-period scores fix the historical architecture choice in each catchment (Ib,s) and globally (Iglobal,s). Late-period evaluation compares the retained instance (Gsame) against independent instances (Gnew) to obtain the retraining loss (D=Gsame-Gnew); comparison with the global rule yields the transferred local increment (ΔGinc,new=Gnew-Gglobal,new). (c) Multi-instance selection (k=1–5): choices are determined by the mean early score of k source instances and evaluated on a disjoint pool of 5 instances.
