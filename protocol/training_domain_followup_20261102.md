# Training-domain follow-up plan

This plan, dated 2026-11-02, followed the initial 107-catchment, 370-catchment and external analyses. It preceded analysis of the new 1999–2003 predictions for the 107 catchments. The plan uses the 30 existing checkpoints and the original weight-selection procedure.

1. **Matched dates.** Use 1999-10-01 to 2003-09-30 for selection and 2003-10-01 to 2008-09-30 for evaluation in both catchment groups. Report retained and independent gains, D, the increment over a global rule using the same early data and the three strategies for the fixed 20-member library. Summarize mean and median NSE, NSE*, KGE and failure counts.
2. **Group contrasts.** Estimate D in the 107 catchments minus D in the 370 with 2,000 synchronized seed/HUC2 draws. Compare ten-year and four-year selection within the same 107 catchments by paired resampling. Report interval precision, including whether the lower bound exceeds zero. The two catchment groups differ in membership.
3. **Record length.** Use all seven overlapping four-water-year windows starting in 1989 through 1995 within the original record and a common 2003–2008 evaluation period. Separate shortening a record from moving it to another period.
4. **Fixed-library strategies.** Report counts below NSE 0 and −1, mean and median NSE and NSE*, and median KGE. Pair each strategy with the pooled ensemble by catchment and count new and resolved failures. Report contributions and deletion sensitivities for the top 1, 3, 5 and 10 late-period gains as measures of concentration. Define an early-risk group from pooled early NSE < 0 and examine its later gains.
5. **Independent splits.** Check the Kratzert et al. (2019) paper, repository and archive for run-level PUB or k-fold simulations. The 50 validation catchments were used in checkpoint selection. Additional spatial splits would require new training and are outside this plan.
6. **External comparisons.** Recalculate NSE* and within-seed catchment ranks under different losses and conceptual benchmarks. Plot observed points by scale and by prespecified comparison category. Identify the asymmetry in semiparametric comparisons where the conceptual model has fixed calibration.
7. **Supplementary reporting.** Put seed-budget planning in the supplement. Retain the FLV median in new summaries and archive earlier outputs. Keep the existing public package distinct from the planned update.
8. **Interpretation.** Training participation and statistical distribution shift are separate properties. Treat underspecification as a testable explanation and assess when historical architecture choices transfer.
